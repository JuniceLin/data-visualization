What the fields mean

  • Caption: the text prompt associated with the image pair.
  • Model 0/1: recorded generators for Images A/B.
  • Label 0/1: human preference weights; this interface translates 1/0 to A selected and 0/1 to B selected.
  • are_different: whether the stored image identifiers differ; it is not a quality score.

Data boundary and limitation

  • Summary: fixed test rows 0–39 retrieved 13 Sep 2026.
  • Gallery: 12 manually selected, classroom-safe labeled rows from that slice.
  • The slice is too small and non-random for general claims about models, users, or prompt alignment.
  • Human choice is contextual and cannot replace semantic, fairness, temporal, or expert evaluation.
  • The inspected mirror card did not state a license; verify terms before reuse beyond this course demonstration.
