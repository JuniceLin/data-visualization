03 · Interpretation boundary

What the evidence can—and cannot—support

Supported here

  • Describe the 17-to-18 choice count among 35 labeled rows in this fixed slice.
  • Inspect the prompt, paired images, model labels, choice weights, date, and exact source row.
  • Compare visible examples as individual records.

Not supported here

  • No “best model,” population preference, objective quality, fairness, or prompt-alignment claim.
  • No conclusion from the 12 curated examples about the full dataset.
  • No image-to-video claim about motion or cross-frame coherence.
