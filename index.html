04 · Interpretation boundary

What each evidence layer can support

Supported here

  • Explore a disclosed systematic sample of official prepared DKMap Pick-a-Pic mapping coordinates colored by CLIPScore.
  • Compare official supplementary MAE values and documented user-study results.
  • Describe the 17-to-18 preference count among 35 labeled rows in the separate fixed slice.

Not supported here

  • No claim that the 2,500 drawn points reproduce the full DKMap system, contours, or training.
  • No “best model,” objective image quality, population preference, fairness, or learning-impact claim.
  • No video claim about motion, editing rhythm, or cross-frame consistency.
