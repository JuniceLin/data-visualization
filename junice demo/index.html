Dataset-specific observation, not a causal claim

In 2,299 rows with both measures, the preferred winner differed from the prompt-alignment winner in 999 rows (43.5%).

In plain language: the video people preferred was not always the video that matched the prompt better.

Limitations

This dataset covers only 79 English prompts and five model labels from 2024–25. Weighted human judgments are subjective; recruitment and weighting details are limited; pair counts are uneven; and some scores are missing. Results do not establish a universal model ranking.
